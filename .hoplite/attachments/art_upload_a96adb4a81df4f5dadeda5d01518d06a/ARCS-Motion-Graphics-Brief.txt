## 2. Full voice-over (standalone — copy this block)

> Before you lock tokens into ARCS PrimePool, pause. Five practical checks separate a careful deposit from a costly mistake.  
>  
> One—confirm the official source. Start at arcs-chain.com, then open the official PrimePool guide from ARCS channels. Bookmark the real domain. Never follow a random DM, airdrop, or look-alike site.  
>  
> Two—confirm network and assets. The guide requires MetaMask, ARCS tokens, ticker ARX, on Ethereum, and enough ETH for gas. The wrong network or token can cause failed transfers or loss.  
>  
> Three—understand Approve, then Deposit. Approval only grants a spending allowance. Deposit is a separate transaction that locks your tokens. Read every wallet prompt before you sign.  
>  
> Four—know the lock. According to the guide, there is no early withdrawal. Check the current lock terms before depositing.  
>  
> Five—read the reward terms in the official app yourself. Subtract gas from any expected return. Factor price risk and smart-contract risk. For small amounts, gas alone can exceed any reward.  
>  
> ARCS HQ announced PrimePool live. Always re-check current terms in the official linked app before you act. This explains how it works—not financial advice.

**Pacing:** Aim for approximately 80–90 seconds with clear pauses. Timings are provisional; measure the final narration and retime scenes rather than rushing. Add a 2–3 second end hold.  
**VO direction:** Neutral, unhurried; stress “Approve, then Deposit,” “no early withdrawal,” “not financial advice.” No sales cadence.

## 4. Art direction

- **Look:** Premium editorial vector + light isometric depth; precise icons; paper-like cards. Avoid neon “crypto moon” tropes, particle spam, fake charts.  
- **Palette:** Ink `#0B0E14` · slate `#1A2230` · frost `#E8EEF7` · teal `#2EC4B6` · amber `#F4A261` · muted danger `#E76F51`.  
- **Type:** One licensed geometric sans for titles; mono for micro-labels. Hierarchy: title > check # > body.  
- **Safe areas:** 5% margins (16:9); 9:16 keeps type clear of platform chrome. **Recompose** 9:16—do not crop.  
- **Audio:** Licensed bed; duck −8 to −12 dB under VO; deliver SRT (+ VO stem if feasible).
